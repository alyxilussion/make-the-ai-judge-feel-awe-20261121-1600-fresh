Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel awe"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600): a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`: rain tapping on a tin roof, a river carrying one leaf a little further, and a night going on, wide and unhurried, asking nothing of you.
